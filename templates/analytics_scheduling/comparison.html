Priority Scheduling

Each complaint category has a priority number (1=Critical, 4=Low). Complaints with lower numbers are served first, regardless of when they were submitted. Matches preemptive priority scheduling in OS.

Shortest Job First (SJF)

Complaint whose category historically takes the fewest hours to resolve is served first. Minimises average waiting time. Matches SJF / SRTF in OS.

First Come First Served

Oldest complaint is served first. Simple and fair but can cause priority inversion (a critical complaint waits behind older low-priority ones). Baseline algorithm for comparison.
